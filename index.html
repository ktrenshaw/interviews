Standing rules

  1. Book the day the invite arrives. Slots go first-come, and a date you hesitate on is usually the one that disappears.
  2. Protect the scarce programs. The Squeezed panel names whoever is down to their last free day.
  3. Move the flexible program, not the scarce one. If two collide, keep the program with fewer remaining dates and ask the other for its alternate — that request is routine in the first week.
  4. Keep a buffer.
  5. Watch the evening before. Most programs hold a dinner the night before, so a same-city back-to-back is fine and a cross-country one is not.
  6. Aim top picks at the middle. Early interviews catch you unpractised and late ones catch you worn out — but this never outranks taking the interview at all.
  7. Decline early, not late. Below about 20% likelihood, take the interview only if the day is still free.
